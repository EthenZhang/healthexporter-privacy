Privacy Policy

Health Data Export · Last updated 2026-08-10

Health Data Export processes Apple Health data on the user's iPhone solely to create export files the user requests. It asks for read-only HealthKit access to categories the user explicitly selects. It does not write to or delete Apple Health data.

The app has no developer account service, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or developer-operated health-data server. Health and route data is processed on device and written only to a Files or iCloud Drive folder or share destination chosen by the user. The developer does not receive those files. Export files are readable and may contain sensitive health and location information; users are responsible for the destinations and people with whom they share them.

The app stores preferences, random collection and installation identifiers, checkpoints, and health-value-free diagnostic records locally or inside the selected export collection. Users can delete exported files by date range without affecting Apple Health. Removing the app does not remove files already saved outside its container.

Apple may process iCloud Drive, HealthKit, crash reports, and App Store data under Apple's own privacy terms.

Data the App Handles

  • Read-only Apple Health data for categories you explicitly select.
  • Workout routes (GPX) for workouts whose route data the system provides.
  • Local preferences such as your selected categories, schedule, and chosen folder bookmark.
  • A random installation identifier and a random collection identifier for takeover logic.
  • SHA-256 manifest checksums and anchors written next to your export files.

Data the App Does Not Collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • No developer-operated server. Nothing you export leaves your device except into the destination you choose.
  • No diagnostics payloads that include health values, device names, or coordinates.

Your Control

  • Choose which categories to read, and change the selection at any time.
  • Choose the destination folder, and revoke the folder bookmark at any time.
  • Delete exported files by date range without affecting Apple Health.
  • Remove the app to stop future exports. Files already saved outside the app remain where you put them.
