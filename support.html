Common Questions

Where do my exports go?
Wherever you point the document picker — a local folder in Files, an iCloud Drive folder, or a third-party Files provider. The app never uploads to a developer server.

Why does the app ask for Health permission?
To read the categories you explicitly select. The app requests read-only access and passes an empty write set to HealthKit.

Will background export run on time?
iOS controls background execution. The app makes a best-effort request and resumes pending work whenever conditions allow. Locked devices and protected Health data may delay exports; this is by design.

What happens to my exports if I delete the app?
Files already saved outside the app stay where you put them. Removing the app only clears its own preferences and local staging; your Apple Health data is untouched.
